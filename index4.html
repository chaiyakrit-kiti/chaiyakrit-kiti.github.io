My Purpose

The Spark That Started It All

When I assembled my first Gundam model in Grade 7, I didn't just see a toy, I saw potential. I started pulling it apart, wondering "How can I make this thing walk?" My father said, "Inventors must dare to dissect." That moment sparked my lifelong fascination with technology.


My Journey & Readiness for ICT

From tinkering with Redstone logic in Minecraft to building my first LED-blinking Arduino prototype, my curiosity led me to coding and electronics. I taught myself C++ and Python, built Smart4Alert,a real-time IoT fire alert system, and won 1st Runner-Up at RMUTK's National Applied Technology Exhibition. That victory showed me innovation's power to protect lives.


Why ICT Mahidol is My Next Step

I chose Mahidol University's Faculty of Information and Communication Technology because its curriculum perfectly matches my path. The combination of programming, cybersecurity, multimedia, and English-taught instruction mirrors my learning style, experimentation and global collaboration. I've strengthened my English through game-based communication, achieved C2 level on EF SET, and placed first in the regional English Genius Test. More importantly, I've applied these skills beyond classroom, leading workshops, teaching IoT to middle schoolers, and representing my school at national cybersecurity camps.


My Career Vision & Ambition

In five years, I aim to deepen my expertise in cybersecurity and interactive media, building systems that are innovative, secure, and meaningful—solutions merging utility with creativity. My long-term goal is creating educational platforms that blend AI, UX, and security to make learning accessible and safe for everyone.


What I Hope to Build – Together

At Mahidol ICT, I plan to contribute as both student and leader. I want to establish a Gundam-Robotics Club to help peers learn microcontroller programming through hands-on projects and support juniors preparing for CTF competitions, building a culture of curiosity and collaboration.


This dream started with a toy. Today, I'm ready to turn that spark into systems that serve society.
